# Private Equity Management Fee Offset Audit

Recalculate management fees and contractual offsets to protect limited partners from excess fund charges.

**Primary buyer:** Limited partners and fund finance teams. **Evidence:** limited partnership agreements, commitments, invested capital, portfolio fees, transaction fees, monitoring fees, offsets, waivers, invoices, capital accounts, and statements.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- LPA fee terms
- Investor commitment registry
- Investment period control
- Fee base calculation
- Step-down calculation
- Transaction fee ingestion
- Monitoring fee ingestion
- Director fee ingestion
- Offset percentage calculation
- Broken-deal expense review
- Waiver and rebate control
- Capital account allocation
- Quarterly statement audit
- GP correction workflow
- Fund investor analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4673>. API: `5673`.
